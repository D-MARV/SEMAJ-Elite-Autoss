# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Project Name:** SEMAJ Elite Autos Web Platform

**Document Version:** 1.0

**Target Release:** Q4 2026

**Document Owner:** SEMAJ Elite Autos Product & Engineering Team

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

The **SEMAJ Elite Autos Web Platform** is a high-performance, minimalist digital showroom designed to bridge trust, luxury, and digital convenience for automotive buyers in Nigeria. Built on a lightweight, motion-rich architecture, the application streamlines vehicle browsing, instant search filtering, multimedia previewing (including engine startup audio and auto-swiping car angles), and direct WhatsApp conversion.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Core User Stories

### Target Audience

* **Primary:** Nigerian automotive buyers (executives, working professionals, business owners) seeking verified foreign-used (*Tokunbo*) or high-grade pre-owned vehicles with zero hidden agent markups.
* **Secondary:** Out-of-state buyers (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan) requiring remote vehicle inspection, transparent customs verification, and bespoke sourcing.

### Core User Stories

1. **Instant Search & Discovery:** *As a buyer searching for a specific brand (e.g., Mercedes-Benz), I want the showroom list to filter live on every keystroke so that I can instantly see relevant inventory without reloading pages.*
2. **Visual & Audio Inspection:** *As a buyer evaluating a car remotely, I want to see an auto-sliding preview of its front, rear, and interior views, as well as listen to its actual engine startup sound, so that I can feel confident before scheduling an in-person physical inspection.*
3. **Transparent Sourcing & Founder Trust:** *As a cautious buyer wary of automotive fraud, I want clear access to the founder’s standards and direct WhatsApp links so I can communicate straight with the primary dealer.*

## 3. Product Features & Functional Requirements

### 3.1. Design System & Global Interaction Physics

* **Custom Crimson Red Cursor System:**
* Replaces default desktop cursor with an active target reticle: an outer ring (`18px` radius) surrounding a centered solid dot (`4px` radius) in Crimson Red (`#D60000`).
* Expands ring with magnetic physics on interactive elements; scales down inner dot on hover.


* **Curved Visual Geometry:**
* Main cards, viewports, and modals feature a `28px` border radius to match the sweeping car silhouette in the SEMAJ logo.


* Control inputs, search bars, and buttons feature full pill geometry (`rounded-full`).


* **Continuous Diagonal Light-Shimmer FX:**
* All primary CTAs (*"Talk to Dealer"*, *"Request Custom Order"*, *"Inspect on WhatsApp"*) and vehicle price badges display a continuous, subtle diagonal light streak sweeping left-to-right across the element in a loop.


* **Micro-Bounce Hover FX:**
* Price tags and primary CTA buttons execute a subtle vertical spring bounce (`translateY(-4px)`) on hover.

### 3.3. Page Specifications

#### Page 1: Home Page (`/`)

1. **Sticky Navigation Bar:**
* Contains SEMAJ Elite Autos logo (links to `/`), navigation links (`[Home]`, `[Showroom]`, `[Custom Sourcing]`, `[Founder]`, `[Reviews]`, `[Contact]`), and a **"Talk to Dealer"** WhatsApp CTA button featuring shimmer FX.




2. **Hero Section:**
* Dynamic 4-second fade transition looping through 3 background visuals: high-res exterior shot, showroom yard alignment, and founder vehicle inspection.
* Overlay gradient vignette ensuring high contrast for hero text (*"PREMIUM DRIVES. UNCOMPROMISED QUALITY."*).
* Primary CTA button: **"Explore Showroom"** (Navigates to `/showroom`).


3. **Bespoke Vehicle Sourcing Banner:**
* Curved `24px` card highlighting custom sourcing capabilities.
* CTA: **"Request Custom Order"** (Triggers WhatsApp chat pre-filled with custom order parameters).


4. **Client Experience Feed (Happy Customers):**
* Single-card vertical crossfade feed cycling through 6–7 real buyer testimonials every 5 seconds (pauses on hover).


5. **About the Founder (Semaj) Section:**
* Left: 3-image auto-sliding container (`3-second` interval loop) showing Semaj conducting engine checks, standing in showroom, and delivering vehicles.
* Right: Summary quality guarantee statement and **"Read Founder's Story"** CTA button.


6. **Founder's Story Modal Overlay:**
* In-page `28px` rounded modal detailing vehicle history verification, zero-markup pricing, and original customs duty guarantees.



#### Page 2: Dedicated Showroom Page (`/showroom`)

1. **Live Search & Filter Control Bar:**
* **Instant Search Input:** Sub-string matching updates the grid on every keyup event without page reloads. Matching characters highlight in Crimson Red (`#D60000`).
* **Status Tabs:** Filter by `All Vehicles`, `Available`, `On Hold`, and `Recently Sold`.
* **Brand Filter Pills:** Quick filters for `Toyota`, `Lexus`, `Mercedes-Benz`, `Honda`, `Hyundai`.


2. **Interactive Vehicle Grid (3 Columns Desktop / 1 Column Mobile):**
* **Top 75% Viewport:** Auto-swipes through `Front View` $\rightarrow$ `Rear View` $\rightarrow$ `Interior View` every 3.5 seconds with horizontal slide transitions.
* **Bottom 25% Title Area:** Displays vehicle title ONLY (e.g., `2012 MERCEDES-BENZ C300`). Price and complete specs are hidden until clicked to preserve a clean layout.
* **Hover Interaction:** Card lifts `8px` (`translateY(-8px)`), drop-shadow expands (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`), and top edge highlights with a Crimson Red stroke.


3. **In-Page Vehicle Detail Modal Overlay:**
* Triggers on vehicle card click with a glass blur backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
* **Left Area (60%):** Manual & 3.5s auto-swiping high-res gallery + **Engine Startup Sound Player Widget** (Plays recorded vehicle ignition audio).
* **Right Area (40%):** Technical specifications (Engine, Transmission, Mileage, Customs Duty), **Shimmering Price Tag Badge** (e.g., `₦13,500,000`), and primary **"Inspect on WhatsApp"** CTA button.

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements (Performance & Compatibility)

* **Performance & Speed:**
* Initial page load under `1.5 seconds` on 4G networks.
* Instant search filtering response time $< 50\text{ms}$ (client-side state evaluation).


* **Responsive Breakpoints:**
* Desktop ($> 1024\text{px}$): Full 3-column vehicle grid, custom reticle cursor enabled.
* Tablet ($768\text{px} - 1023\text{px}$): 2-column grid.
* Mobile ($< 767\text{px}$): Single column layout, touch-swipe gestures enabled for media cards, standard touch pointer fallback.


* **Audio Optimization:**
* Engine audio clips formatted in compressed `.mp3` ($< 300\text{KB}$ per file) with lazy-loading triggered only when the detail modal opens.



---

## 6. Verification & Acceptance Criteria

1. **Search Criteria:** Typing any character string in the search bar immediately filters the grid without page flicker or full re-renders.
2. **Carousel Criteria:** Hovering over any card pauses the 3.5s auto-swipe timer; moving the mouse away resumes the loop seamlessly.
3. **Audio Criteria:** Clicking the engine startup toggle plays audio without disrupting active background timers or carousel transitions.
4. **WhatsApp Integration:** Clicking any WhatsApp CTA successfully passes pre-formatted vehicle details (Title, Price, Duty Status) straight into the WhatsApp conversation interface.
